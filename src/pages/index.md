# Francisco Villacis

PhD student in mathematics at the University of Waterloo.

## About
My research lies at the intersection of geometry and combinatorics, with connections to mathematical physics. I study how combinatorial structures, such as matroids and polyhedral decompositions, encode geometric information.

## Current research
I am currently studying images of subvarieties of projective toric varieties under moment maps. These images are called ``compactified amoebas’’ and live inside the moment polytope of the projective variety. For linear subspaces of projective space, I investigate how much of the compactified amoeba is determined by the associated matroid and related combinatorial data.
