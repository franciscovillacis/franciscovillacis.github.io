How to explore

Choose an image

Select a Grassmannian type and a starting configuration. The colored set is the moment image inside the standard simplex.

Move a circuit point

Drag a marked point along its supporting edge or face. The other circuit points adjust to keep the image compatible. You can also select a point and use its coordinate sliders with the arrow keys.

Look around

Drag empty space to rotate and scroll to zoom. On a touchscreen, use one finger to rotate and pinch to zoom. Reset the view to return to the starting camera.

Explore special configurations

The starting configurations include lower-dimensional images. For Gr(3,4), the phase control lets you explore the transition between real and complex configurations.
